About Hotel Shree Yash Residency

Where generations meet over thalis, dosas, and festival favorites.

Our Story

Hotel Shree Yash Residency is a family restaurant rooted in Wai’s culture and the surrounding Satara district. We began with a simple promise: serve food that tastes like home — whether it is a crisp dosa, a spicy Veg Kolhapuri, or a humble plate of Sabudana on a fasting day. Our guests are neighbors, travelers, and families celebrating milestones together.

Traditional Taste

We honor traditional recipes — slow-cooked gravies, freshly ground masalas, and breads rolled to order. South Indian breakfast items share the menu with Maharashtrian mains so everyone at the table finds something they love. Quality ingredients and consistent seasoning are our daily ritual.

Festival & Seasonal Menus

Throughout the year we prepare festival-based special menus. During Shravan and other auspicious periods, enjoy Upwas-friendly dishes, modak, dahi vada, and thalis that respect tradition without compromising flavor. Ask our staff what is special this week — we rotate sweets and combos with the calendar.
